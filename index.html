The rules this page uses

Defaults come from DIAT's Examination Policies & Procedures (Sep 2025), §5.2, §5.5 and §6. If your department says otherwise, change the numbers here and every target updates straight away.

  • Per course: 2 Assessment Tests at 20 marks each, Internal (assignments & quizzes) 10 marks, End-sem 50 marks.
  • To pass: at least 40% in the end-sem and at least 40% in continuous evaluation, each counted separately. There are no grace marks.
  • You must earn at least 75% of first-year credits to register for semester 3.
  • Not covered here: PGC601/602 Research Methodology (one test and an 80-mark end-sem), self-study courses (100-mark end-sem) and labs.
