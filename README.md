# DIAT Target Marks

**Live:** https://diat-target-marks.vercel.app

A small, unofficial helper for DIAT Pune M.Tech students. Enter the tests you've written, guess the professor's mood for the 10-mark internal, and see exactly what you need in the remaining tests and the end-sem, subject by subject. Includes a CGPA calculator and a downloadable target sheet.

Rules are taken from DIAT's [Examination Policies & Procedures (Sep 2025)](https://diat.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/ExamPolicy-Sep2025-Printed.pdf):

- Per course: 2 Assessment Tests at 20 marks each, Internal (assignments & quizzes) 10 marks, End-sem 50 marks
- Pass: at least 40% in continuous evaluation **and** at least 40% in the end-sem, each counted separately
- Grades: O 91+, A+ 81+, A 71+, B+ 61+, B 51+, C 41+, P 40
- CGPA to percentage: (CGPA − 0.75) × 10

Everything runs in the browser. Marks never leave your device; the site counts page views only.
